      Exercise from Garmin (via Strava)

      Workouts recorded on your Garmin sync to Strava, and MealTrack pulls them in and adds the calories burned to that day's budget.

      One-time setup (about 5 minutes)
      1. In the Garmin Connect app, make sure Strava is linked: More → Settings → Connected Apps → Strava.
      2. In a web browser, sign in at strava.com/settings/api and create an app:
        • Application Name: MealTrack (anything works)
        • Category: Other
        • Website:
        • Authorization Callback Domain:
        Strava may ask you to upload an icon. Any image is fine.
      3. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret it shows into the boxes below, then tap Connect Strava and approve access.

      Your secret is stored only on this phone (and in your backups). Each person sets up their own Strava app, because Strava lets a new app connect only its owner.

      Watch calorie estimates often run high, so many people count 50–75%.

      Restaurant search

      Restaurant and everyday foods come from the USDA FoodData Central database. Without a key the app shares a demo key that allows only about 30 searches an hour. A free personal key removes that limit: get one here (takes a minute, sent by email).

      Backup & moving phones

      Your diary lives only on this phone. A backup is a single file holding your diary, foods, goals and settings.

      How to move to a new phone
      1. On your old phone, tap Save a backup and send the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone: email it to yourself, or save it to iCloud Drive / Google Drive / Files.
      2. On your new phone, open the MealTrack link, add it to your home screen, and open it.
      3. Go to Settings → Restore from a backup and pick the file.

      Restoring replaces everything on that phone with the backup's contents.
